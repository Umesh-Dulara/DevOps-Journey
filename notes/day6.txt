6. What are log levels? List them in order of severity and explain when each is used. (Hint: think ERROR, WARN, INFO, DEBUG...)

log levels are the classification of software and system events by their importance,severity and urgencey.
*TRACE: extremely fine graded,step by step code execution details.Used for deep debugging.
*DEBUG: Diagnostic details. Used in test environments
*INFO: standard log level that indicates that something has happened.Used in standard production environments
*WARN: Unexpected or abnormal events that do not stop the operation.Standard production enviorenments to flag non fatal deviations.
*ERROR: Functional failures where a specific operation or request failed to complete the task.Used in high priority production logging.
*FATAL: is used for system-wide, catastropic crashes where a critical component completely halts or cannot recover, forcing the entire program to stap.Used to immediately inform developers.

9. In a real production environment with 50 servers all generating logs, reading individual log files on each machine would be impossible. How do companies solve this? What does a centralised logging system look like?

they keep a seperated server for the logs.This is called centralized log management(CLM).Instead of storing logs in each device they use log shippers to dump the logs of individual device to an external storage in real time.
CLM is look like this
individual server>log shippers>central pileline>storage and analytics
every server runs a lightweight background agent called log shipper to read local log streams instantly and securely forward them across the network.But when so many servers simultaneously dumps their logs the central logging infrastructure can be overwhelmed.So the companies use message brockers for que the logs so data doesnt dropped during high traffic hours.Then the logs are parsed structured,indexed and stored in highly scalable databases.companies use two storing methods as hot storage and cold storage to save money.the hot storage is fast but small in size they are used to store the most recent logs for specific time period.(for last 30 days of logs).cold storage is slow and used to store compressed old data.
